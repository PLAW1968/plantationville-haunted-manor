Last year, they gathered in the shadows. This Halloween, The Coven returns to PlantationVille Haunted Manor.

The witches are stirring once again, and this year their magic has grown stronger. Expect bubbling potions, mysterious spells, strange happenings and perhaps a little mischief as The Coven takes its place within the Manor for another Halloween night.

They may look frightening, but our witches know exactly who has wandered into their domain. Younger visitors are treated with a gentler touch, while the braver souls may discover that getting too close to a witch is not always the wisest idea.

What are they planning this year? We're not telling. Some secrets are better left until Halloween night.
